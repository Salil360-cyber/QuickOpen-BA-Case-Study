# 2. Stakeholders and RACI

| Stakeholder | What they need | RACI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Head of Digital Banking (sponsor) | More accounts at lower cost; a clear business case | Accountable |
| Product Owner, onboarding app | A prioritised backlog with clear user stories | Responsible |
| Business Analyst | Requirements, analysis, documents, UAT support | Responsible |
| Compliance / KYC team | Every change stays within RBI KYC rules | Consulted |
| Video KYC operations team | Manageable agent load and staffing data | Consulted |
| IT / development team | Clear, feasible requirements and acceptance criteria | Responsible |
| QA team | Testable requirements, RTM, UAT cases | Consulted |
| Customer support | Fewer complaints; list of common issues | Informed |
| New customers (end users) | A quick, simple account opening | Informed |

## Elicitation plan

| Technique | With whom | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Interviews | Sponsor, Product Owner | Goals, constraints, success measures |
| Workshop | Compliance, VKYC operations, IT | Feasible options within KYC rules |
| Document analysis | Journey data, support tickets | Where and why customers drop |
| Survey | Recent applicants | Pain points in their own words |

> In this case study the stakeholders and interviews are simulated; the journey data analysis is real analysis of synthetic data.
