# 5. Solution options and prioritisation (MoSCoW)

| Solution | MoSCoW | Why | Effort |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Video KYC slot booking | Must | Hits the biggest drop point (3,176) and the 16-30 / 30+ min waits | Medium |
| Reminder 15 min before the call | Must | 1,365 drops are missed calls | Low |
| One-tap rebook of a missed call | Must | Turns a lost customer into a rescheduled one | Low |
| Save and resume journey | Should | Recovers customers who leave at any stage | Medium |
| Ops dashboard of booked slots for staffing | Should | Lets VKYC ops plan evening and night shifts | Low |
| Low-bandwidth VKYC mode | Could | Tier 3 approval gap (75.6% vs 79.7%) | High |
| PAN name-mismatch guidance | Could | Second-largest drop (1,874) but needs more data on causes | Low |
| AI chatbot for document help | Won't (this release) | Basics first; review after launch | High |
