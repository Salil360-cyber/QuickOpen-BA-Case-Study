# 1. Problem statement and goals

## Background

Sahyadri Bank (fictional) lets customers open a zero-balance savings account fully in its mobile app and website. The journey has 7 stages: Mobile OTP → Personal Details → PAN Verification → Aadhaar eKYC → Video KYC → Nominee & Preferences → First Deposit & Activation.

## Problem statement

> Only **52.6%** of customers who start digital account opening complete it (10,526 of 20,000, Apr-Sep 2026). **Video KYC** is the biggest drop point: it loses **3,176** of the 14,577 customers who reach it (**21.8%**). Failure rises from **11.2%** when the agent wait is 5 minutes or less to **45.4%** when it is over 30 minutes, and night slots have the longest waits (27.5 minutes on average). This affects new customers, the digital banking team, the Video KYC operations team and customer support.

## Business goal (SMART)

Raise journey completion from **52.6% to at least 56%** within 6 months of launching the Video KYC improvements, and cut the share of Video KYC calls with a wait over 15 minutes from **34%** to under **15%**.

## Scope

| In scope | Out of scope |
| --- | --- |
| Video KYC scheduling, reminders and wait time | Changing RBI KYC rules or required documents |
| Save and resume of the journey | Core banking system changes |
| Drop-off reporting for the digital team | Marketing and acquisition spend |
| PAN name-mismatch guidance (next phase) | Branch account opening |
